## Summary

A principal individual contributor stays technically grounded while increasing the quality and leverage of decisions across teams without becoming an organizational bottleneck.

## Key Claims

- accepted: Principal individual contributors can lead through deep technical ownership, broad influence, pathfinding, or multi-organization alignment.
  source: raw/sources/advice-for-new-principal-tech-ics.md
- accepted: The role shifts leverage toward vision, review, sponsorship, context sharing, and multiplying other builders.
  source: raw/sources/advice-for-new-principal-tech-ics.md
- accepted: Principals should focus on durable organizational needs that would otherwise remain neglected and that match their strengths.
  source: raw/sources/advice-for-new-principal-tech-ics.md
- accepted: Principal work can span product, design, engineering, science, quality, hiring, finance, and culture when cross-functional judgment is useful.
  source: raw/sources/advice-for-new-principal-tech-ics.md
- accepted: High-leverage principal work can make important but undervalued work visible and actionable.
  source: raw/sources/advice-for-new-principal-tech-ics-fuller-notes.md
- accepted: A principal should reduce organizational dependency on themselves by guiding and unblocking work without owning every critical path.
  source: raw/sources/advice-for-new-principal-tech-ics-fuller-notes.md
- accepted: Owner, sponsor, and consultant roles should be explicit so teams understand decision rights and support expectations.
  source: raw/sources/advice-for-new-principal-tech-ics-fuller-notes.md
- accepted: Seniority amplifies casual comments, silence, and disagreement, so intent, context, uncertainty, and reasoning should be explicit.
  source: raw/sources/advice-for-new-principal-tech-ics-fuller-notes.md
- accepted: Principals should make room for others to speak, decide, and receive credit instead of filling every gap.
  source: raw/sources/advice-for-new-principal-tech-ics-fuller-notes.md
- accepted: Breadth work requires protected thinking time, deliberate scope, peer support, continued learning, and attention to burnout risk.
  source: raw/sources/advice-for-new-principal-tech-ics.md
