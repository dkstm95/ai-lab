## Ingest Focus

This is a paraphrased note, not a copy of the X Article. It preserves the
article's concept of tasks as the unit of model improvement and separates that
concept from its market-size predictions.

## Structured Notes

- Token volume is framed as a legible proxy for AI usage and computation. The
  article proposes tasks as an analogous unit for data-driven model
  improvement.
- In reinforcement learning, a task combines an initial state, an environment
  in which the model acts, and a reward signal or verifier that scores the
  behavior. The article uses "task" more broadly for units of model-improvement
  data.
- As general internet data and simple capabilities become less differentiating,
  the article expects incremental high-quality, domain-specific data to become
  a bottleneck.
- Expert work is not represented by documents alone. A legal task, for
  example, can require a realistic prompt, access to a relevant environment,
  an expert process, and a rubric that verifies the resulting work.
- More expert rubrics, vertical agents, longer-horizon work, and enterprise
  evaluations all increase the number, complexity, and cost of useful tasks.
- The article predicts a large market for mobilizing experts to externalize
  tacit knowledge into datasets, evaluations, and reinforcement-learning
  environments across professional domains.
- Its strategic claim is that applied AI companies and enterprises can
  differentiate from off-the-shelf models by building task and evaluation
  infrastructure around their own domains.

## Source Limits

The article is a venture-market thesis, not a neutral market study. Everett
Randle is a Benchmark partner and uses Benchmark portfolio company Mercor as
the primary market example. Claims about lab spending, customer spending,
Mercor revenue, the share of relevant knowledge held in people's heads, and a
future trillion-dollar category were not independently verified here.
Algorithmic improvements are explicitly excluded from the thesis.
